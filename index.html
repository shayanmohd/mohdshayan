About Me

Engineering, Enterprise & Empathy

An engineer who became a founder, and a founder who builds for the people technology too often leaves behind

I'm a product engineer and CS graduate from Delhi Technological University who turned a love of building into an enterprise. As founder of SocialSure Private Limited, I lead an AI-first company shaping the next generation of intelligent products.

But code is only half the story. I'm a committed philanthropist, directing time and resources toward children in need—from education and nutrition to access to technology. I believe progress is hollow unless it reaches the people who need it most.

That conviction is recognised in my fellowships of the Royal Society of Arts (FRSA) and the Royal Geographical Society (FRGS)—two communities devoted to social progress, ideas, and a deeper understanding of our world.
