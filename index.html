About

Engineering, Enterprise & Empathy

An engineer who became a founder, and a founder who builds for the people technology too often leaves behind.

Mohd Shayan is a product engineer and CS graduate from Delhi Technological University who turned a love of building into an enterprise. As founder of SocialSure Private Limited, he leads an AI-first company shaping the next generation of intelligent products.

But code is only half the story. He is a committed philanthropist, directing time and resources toward children in need, from education and nutrition to access to technology. He believes progress is hollow unless it reaches the people who need it most.

That conviction is recognised in his fellowships of the Royal Society of Arts (FRSA) and the Royal Geographical Society (FRGS), two communities devoted to social progress, ideas, and a deeper understanding of the world.

SocialSure Private Limited

Founder & CEO · AI-First Company

SocialSure is Mohd Shayan's AI venture, a company spanning multiple interests across artificial intelligence, built to make intelligent technology genuinely useful, accessible, and human. As founder, he sets the product vision, leads engineering, and steers the strategy that takes ideas from prototype to production.

The company's work runs across three fronts: AI training, an AI-powered social media ecosystem, and a growing suite of AI-driven products, united by one conviction: technology should create opportunity, not concentrate it.

Philanthropy

Giving Back to Children in Need

The measure of progress is who it lifts along the way.

Alongside his work in technology, Mohd Shayan dedicates time and resources to the children who need it most. He supports causes that open doors for young people, because talent is universal, but opportunity is not.

From funding education and nutrition to bridging the digital divide, his goal is simple: help more children get a fair start, and a real chance to build the future on their own terms.

“Build things that matter, and make sure they reach the people who matter most.”
