The order things happen in

The options always run in the same seven steps, whichever you tick first. That is why trimmed lines dedupe properly, and why line numbers run 1, 2, 3 down the finished list.

  1. 1

    Trim whitespace

    Spaces and tabs at the start and end of every line are removed, so the steps that follow see the bare text.

  2. 2

    Remove empty lines

    Lines with nothing in them, or nothing but spaces and tabs, are dropped and counted as empty removed.

  3. 3

    Remove duplicates

    The first copy of each line stays where it is and later copies are dropped, compared with or without case and surrounding whitespace.

  4. 4

    Sort

    A to Z, Z to A, by length, naturally so that file2 comes before file10, or shuffled with the browser's secure random number generator. Lines that tie keep their order.

  5. 5

    Reverse

    The whole list is turned upside down, after any sort, so the last line comes first.

  6. 6

    Number

    Each line gets its position in the finished order, written as 1. , 2. and so on.

  7. 7

    Prefix and suffix

    Your prefix goes in front of every line and your suffix after it, outside the line number, so a prefix of - gives - 1. apple.
