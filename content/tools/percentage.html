What is X% of Y?

A share of a number: a 15% tip on a bill of 200, or 18% tax on 1,000.

%

15% of 200

30

200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30

X is what percent of Y?

A part as a share of the whole: marks out of a total, or sales from one product out of all sales.

30 as a percentage of 200

15%

30 ÷ 200 × 100 = 15

Percentage change from A to B

How much a figure has risen or fallen, measured against where it started. Negative values are fine: the change is compared with the size of the starting value, so it always reads the right way up.

Change from 200 to 250

25% increase

(250 − 200) ÷ 200 × 100 = 25

Increase or decrease Y by X%

Both answers at once: the number after a rise of that percentage, and after a cut of the same percentage. A price after tax, or after a discount.

%

200 increased by 15%

230

200 decreased by 15%

170

15% of 200 = 30 · 200 + 30 = 230 · 200 − 30 = 170

Y is X% of what?

The whole, when you know a part and the percentage it makes up: the price before tax behind a total, or the full mark when 30 marks scored 15%.
