Let's Talk

Free tools

Small, fast utilities, free for anyone to use. Each one runs entirely in your browser and lives at its own address.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. What you type, paste or drop in stays on your device.

Free, with no catch

No sign-up, no watermark, no limits, and no trial that runs out.

Open source

The code behind every tool is public on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does.

Writing

  • deAIfy deaify.mohdshayan.com Strip em dashes, curly quotes, hidden characters and stock AI phrases from any text, with a live diff of every change. Open

Business & money

  • Commission Calculator commission.mohdshayan.com Flat or tiered plans with base pay and splits, plus a reverse mode that finds the sales needed to hit a target. Open
  • GST Calculator gst.mohdshayan.com Add or remove GST at any rate, with the CGST, SGST and IGST split ready for an invoice. Open
  • EMI Calculator emi.mohdshayan.com Monthly EMI, total interest, a full amortisation schedule, and exactly what prepaying saves. Open
  • Margin Calculator margin.mohdshayan.com Margin, markup and profit from cost and price, or the selling price you need for a target margin. Open

Everyday utilities
